🏫 Smart College Complaint System

A smart and simple platform for students to report, track, and resolve college complaints efficiently.

How It Works

📝 1. Submit

Students submit their college complaint online.

🤖 2. Classify

The system automatically identifies the category and priority.

🔍 3. Track

Students can track their complaint status using the Complaint ID.

✅ 4. Resolve

Admins manage complaints and update their status.
